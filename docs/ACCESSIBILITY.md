# Accessibility and Reader Agency

**Status:** Product requirements and open design work; implementation coverage is in [TECHNICAL_SPEC.md](TECHNICAL_SPEC.md).

- Preserve a static traditional reading path. Motion, audio, haptics, and interaction enrich a story but should not be prerequisites to read it unless clearly disclosed as essential to a specific work.
- Offer reduced motion, muted audio, captions/transcripts, descriptions of meaningful images, keyboard and assistive input, readable timing, and reader control of auto-prompt delay.
- Treat handedness as an editable ergonomic preference, not an assumption about ability. Contextual touch controls need accessible alternatives and must avoid obscuring content.
- Haptic patterns may reinforce thunder or footsteps, including for deaf readers, but essential meaning also needs a visible or textual representation and a way to disable vibration.
- Achievement notices should start as a quiet checked icon, expand only by choice, and be available to screen readers without interrupting active reading.
- Age-related onboarding is motivated by content filtering and age-appropriate discovery. A playful multiple-choice “adult test” is not reliable age verification. Specify age bands, content labels, privacy, and any actual assurance method only after policy and legal review.
