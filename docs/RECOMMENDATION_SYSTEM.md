# Recommendation and Discovery

**Status:** Product direction; ranking and policy remain unimplemented.

Discovery should match readers with stories they may value while giving new and unusual creators a chance. Candidate signals include genres, themes, creator, presentation style, completion, saves, follows, and genuine appreciation. Do not optimize primarily for time spent, compulsive returns, or raw clicks.

## Exploration concepts

- “Try Something New” offers a clearly labeled path outside familiar genres.
- “Hidden Gems” and “New Creators” expose strong work without requiring a large existing audience.
- A traditional section may help readers find static paged and vertical works, without making it a lesser format.
- Reader achievements can introduce zoom, auto-prompt, backward navigation, and diverse genres. Community and exploration achievements can recognize constructive participation and discovering debut creators. Participation should remain optional.
- Content labels and reader controls should shape age-appropriate filtering. The aim of any age assurance is content access, not gamified proof of maturity.

Ranking details, consent, fairness checks, cold-start handling, and moderation rules require separate proposals before implementation.
